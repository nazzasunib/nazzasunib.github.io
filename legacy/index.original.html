GUB 2026 — AI & Career Guidance Seminar

Empowering Students for an AI-Driven Career Future

The rapid growth of Artificial Intelligence is transforming the way we learn, work and build our careers. To help students understand these changes and prepare for the future of work, NidusLab organized an AI & Career Guidance Seminar 2026 at Green University of Bangladesh in collaboration with the Center for Career Development (CCD).

The seminar brought together university students who were eager to learn how AI is changing the job market, what skills employers are looking for and how they can prepare themselves for a competitive career landscape.

A Day of Learning, Networking and Career Exploration

The day started early with the NidusLab team setting up a dedicated booth on campus from 9:00 AM. Students visited the booth throughout the day, where they learned more about NidusLab, NidusJob and the opportunities available through AI-powered career solutions.

The main seminar took place from 2:00 PM to 4:30 PM at the Green University campus. The session focused on the growing impact of Artificial Intelligence on careers and the importance of continuously developing relevant skills.

Students were introduced to practical insights on topics such as:

  • The impact of AI on the future job market
  • How AI is changing traditional career paths
  • Skills students should develop for the future
  • How to use AI as a career development tool
  • Building a stronger and more competitive profile
  • Preparing for AI-powered recruitment and hiring
  • Understanding the changing expectations of employers

Connecting Students with the Future of Recruitment

One of the key focuses of the seminar was helping students understand how technology is changing the recruitment process.

Through NidusJob, students can explore AI-powered career features designed to make job searching smarter and more personalized. From matching candidates with suitable opportunities to improving resumes and identifying skill gaps, AI can play an important role in helping job seekers make better career decisions.

The seminar gave students an opportunity to understand that AI is not simply a technology for the future — it is already becoming an important part of today's workplace.

More Than Just a Seminar

For NidusLab, the event was more than a single seminar. It was an opportunity to connect directly with students, understand their career concerns and create meaningful conversations around the future of employment.

The booth provided students with a chance to interact with the NidusLab team, learn about the company's technology-driven ecosystem and explore how platforms like NidusJob can support them throughout their career journey.

The enthusiasm and participation of the students made the experience especially meaningful.

Building a Smarter Career Ecosystem

As technology continues to evolve, students need more than academic knowledge to succeed. They need adaptability, digital skills, communication abilities, problem-solving skills and the confidence to continuously learn.

At NidusLab, we believe that technology should make these opportunities more accessible.

Through initiatives like the AI & Career Guidance Seminar, NidusLab aims to bridge the gap between students, technology and the evolving job market — helping young professionals become better prepared for the future.

Looking Ahead

The Green University seminar was an inspiring step toward creating stronger connections between the education ecosystem and the rapidly changing world of technology and employment.

We are grateful to Green University of Bangladesh and the Center for Career Development (CCD) for their support and collaboration and to all the students who joined the seminar, visited our booth and actively participated in the discussions.

The future of work is changing.

And the next generation needs to be ready for it.

NidusLab — Building Smart Solutions.

Discovering NidusJob: A Smarter Way to Build Your Career

A key part of the discussion was the introduction of NidusJob, NidusLab's AI-powered job marketplace designed to make the job-search and recruitment experience smarter, faster and more personalized.

For students and job seekers, finding the right opportunity can often be challenging. From searching through countless job postings to preparing a resume and understanding whether a particular role matches their skills, the traditional job-search process can take significant time and effort.

NidusJob is designed to simplify this journey with the power of AI.

The platform helps job seekers discover opportunities that align with their background and career interests while providing AI-powered tools to strengthen their overall job application process.

Through NidusJob, job seekers can access features such as:

  • AI-Powered Job Matching — Discover job opportunities that match your background and skills
  • CV & Job Match Score — Understand how well your CV aligns with a specific job
  • AI-Based Skills Gap Analysis — Identify the skills you may need to develop for your target roles
  • AI-Built ATS-Friendly Resume — Create a professional resume designed for modern recruitment systems
  • AI-Generated Cover Letter — Generate personalized cover letters for job applications
  • AI-Generated Interview Questions — Prepare for interviews with role-relevant questions
  • Smart Job Alerts — Stay updated with relevant opportunities
  • Application Tracking Dashboard — Keep track of your job applications in one place

The goal is simple: make job searching less complicated and help candidates make smarter career decisions.

For the students at Green University, the introduction to NidusJob offered a practical example of how AI can be used not only for learning and productivity but also for building a stronger career.

As AI continues to transform recruitment, platforms like NidusJob are helping create a more connected ecosystem between talent and opportunity.
